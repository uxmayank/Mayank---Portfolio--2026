My story

From freelance screens to owning a whole product

I started out designing for freelance clients: an AI astrology app, an automotive service website and a resume builder. Each one taught me something different about mobile, web and how people move through a product.

Then I joined Square Foot Story as its only designer. There was no app, no screens and no design language, just a clear problem: homebuyers in Delhi NCR couldn’t tell which builders to trust. Working directly with the founder and developers, I designed the product from a blank canvas to a live app on the Play Store and App Store.

With short deadlines and no other designers, I learned that speed and consistency only work together when there’s a system underneath. So I built one, and set up an AI-first workflow with MCP-ready files that took developer implementation from 3–5 days to the same day.

How I think

Three things I bring to every product

  • 01PM mindset

    Start with the problem

    Before pixels, I want to know whose problem we’re solving and what the business needs. Then I scope what can ship.

  • 02Systems thinking

    Systems over screens

    One good foundation beats fifty beautiful one-offs. Tokens, components and rules keep every screen consistent.

  • 03Implementation

    Design for the build

    A design isn’t done until it ships. Clean structure and naming make files developers, and AI tools, can trust.

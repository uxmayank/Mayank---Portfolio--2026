01The problem

Buying a home in Delhi NCR runs on trust you can’t verify

Homebuyers make one of the biggest financial decisions of their lives with very little reliable information. Three problems sit at the center of it.

  • Problem 01

    Not knowing which builders to trust

    Every builder looks credible in an ad. Buyers have no simple way to check track records, approvals or delivery history.

  • Problem 02

    Delays nobody warns you about

    Possession dates slip quietly. Buyers find out late, often after paying a large share of the price.

  • Problem 03

    Costs that appear at the end

    Stamp duty, registration, circle rates and interiors add up to far more than the listed price.

02Starting point

No product. No design. No system.

When I joined, there was nothing to iterate on: no app, no screens and no design language. As the only designer, working directly with the founder and the developers, I owned the product experience end to end.

04Key features

Three problems, three features

Each core feature answers one of these three problems.

Feature 01Solves: trust

Builder Credibility score

Buyers had no way to judge a builder beyond marketing. A single rating would just be another number to doubt.

Key design decisions

  1. 1Break a 100-point score into 5 weighted factors, so buyers see why a builder scores high or low, not just the number.
  2. 2Explain every factor in a bottom sheet: what data is used, how it moves the score, and its limits.
  3. 3Add plain disclaimers so the score reads as guidance, not a legal verdict.

Feature 02Solves: delays

Meter Square construction tracking

Possession dates slip quietly, and buyers usually find out last. Delay information needed to be impossible to miss.

Key design decisions

  1. 1Show construction progress and an On time or Delayed status directly on every project card.
  2. 2Flag possession date changes with a clear before-and-after tooltip.
  3. 3Let buyers filter by delivery, construction stage, appreciation and builder credibility.

Feature 03Solves: hidden costs

Smart Calculators

The listed price is rarely what a buyer pays. Stamp duty, registration, circle rates and interiors were left for buyers to discover late.

Key design decisions

  1. 1Four calculators for the costs buyers miss: EMI, stamp duty, circle rate and interior cost.
  2. 2Plain-language results instead of finance jargon, with every input visible and editable.
  3. 3Disclaimer sheets that explain why rates vary and how to verify them before deciding.

05How I worked

Shipping a new feature in 2–3 days

New features usually came with a 2–3 day deadline. Instead of cutting corners, I built a workflow that made speed and consistency work together.

  1. 01

    Brief with AI

    Requirements become a clear problem, scope and prompt in ChatGPT or Claude.

  2. 02

    Prototype fast

    Figma Make or Lovable generates a working draft to test the flow early.

  3. 03

    Systemize

    Every element is rebuilt on the design foundation in Figma.

  4. 04

    Ship same day

    MCP-ready files: developers went from 3–5 days to same-day implementation.

08Learnings

What this project taught me

Four ideas I now bring to every product I design.

  • 01

    Design for trust

    In real estate, people don’t act on data they don’t understand. Explaining a score mattered more than the score itself.

  • 02

    Foundation first

    Building the system before scaling screens made every later screen faster and more consistent.

  • 03

    AI drafts, judgment decides

    AI gives a fast first draft. Refining it and fitting it to the product is what makes it feel right.

  • 04

    Design for implementation

    How a Figma file is structured affects how fast it ships as much as how it looks.

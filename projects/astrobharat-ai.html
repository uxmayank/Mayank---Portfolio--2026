An AI astrology companion for everyday spiritual life

AstroBharat AI brings Vedic astrology to the phone: AI-powered kundli insights, horoscope guidance, an astrology shop, a digital e-Mandir and structured learning courses. I designed the app’s UI and UX.

01The product

One app for every spiritual need

Spiritual users usually jump between astrologers, temple visits, shops and courses to get what they need. AstroBharat AI brings all of it into one place, combining AI guidance with access to verified pandits.

  1. 01

    AI kundli & horoscope

    Personalised kundli insights, daily horoscope guidance and an AI astrologer available any time.

  2. 02

    Astro Shop & e-Mandir

    Certified remedies, gemstones and yantras, plus virtual temple visits and pooja booking.

  3. 03

    Learning courses

    Structured foundation and diploma programs in Vedic astrology, palmistry, vastu and numerology.

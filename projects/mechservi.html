A marketplace for car and bike care

MechServi connects vehicle owners with verified vendors for doorstep cleaning, servicing, spare parts and accessories. I designed the marketing website that explains the service, earns trust and brings in both customers and vendors.

What the website needed to do

MechServi serves two audiences at once: car and bike owners looking for reliable service, and vendors who want more customers. The landing page had to explain a new kind of service quickly and give both groups a clear next step.

  1. 01

    Two clear paths

    Explore Marketplace for owners and Join as Vendor for businesses, side by side in the hero.

  2. 02

    Explain the recommendation flow

    A four-step visual shows how MechServi looks at a vehicle, collects preferences and suggests what it needs.

  3. 03

    Build trust early

    Verified vendors, secure payments and customer numbers sit high on the page, before any product pitch.
